Assumptions

  • Fast feedback. The 30-day timeline needs answers to questions within 1 working day and phase reviews within 2 working days.
  • TRCN checks. TRCN does not offer a public API for automatic checks, so verification is done by your team in the admin panel using the uploaded certificate and TRCN number. If you later get API access from TRCN or a verification provider, it can be connected as a separate job.
  • Background checks. The platform records the result and shows the badge. The check itself is done by a third-party provider you choose.
  • Holding lesson payments. Paystack is not a licensed escrow service. Lesson payments are collected into your Paystack balance and paid out to teachers after the lesson is confirmed. Please confirm this approach with your legal or finance adviser.
  • Client items. You provide the logo files, brand colours, a Paystack business account, and accounts for hosting, SendGrid and Termii before Phase 3.
  • Placement fees. Fees are invoiced through the platform when a school marks a teacher as hired. Off-platform hires cannot be tracked automatically.
